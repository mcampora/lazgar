A short note on the operating model we proposed last week.

Dear Mathieu,

Following our workshop on Tuesday, I wanted to put on paper the operating model we sketched on the whiteboard — both so the conversation survives the week, and so your steering committee has something to react to.

The shape we converged on splits delivery from platform: a small product engineering pod owns the customer-facing roadmap, while a separate platform team carries the shared infrastructure, security baseline, and the data layer. Both report into a single technology lead, but their planning cycles are deliberately offset.

Three things to decide before we draft the full proposal: (1) where finance sits in the platform team's intake, (2) whether the data layer absorbs the analytics function we discussed, and (3) the hiring sequence for Q3.

Happy to walk through any of it on Thursday. — Y.
